Welcome to The Mesh Guys

We are a community website dedicated to the creators, developers, and teams behind mesh networking projects across the country. From groups like ChicagoMesh to local mesh communities everywhere, The Mesh Guys highlights the people building decentralized communication networks, sharing knowledge, and growing the mesh ecosystem. Explore community projects, member websites, updates, and resources all in one place.

About The Mesh Guys

The Mesh Guys is a hub for mesh networking communities and creators. Our goal is to connect groups across the country and provide a place where people can share projects, guides, updates, and ideas about decentralized communication systems.
